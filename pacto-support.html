Frequently asked questions

What is Pacto?

Pacto is an app for parents. You create a profile for your child, then write a "pacto": an effort-for-reward agreement. The brief can be sent to your child by message (e.g. WhatsApp) from your own phone. The child does not need to install anything.

Is Pacto free?

Pacto is free to install. You can set up your family profiles and browse all categories for free. Launching a pacto requires a single one-time in-app purchase, "Pacto for Life". There is no subscription. An optional one-time purchase unlocks the Special Birthday Day feature.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the paywall screen and tap "Restore purchases". Use the same Apple ID that made the purchase. You can also manage purchases in your Apple ID settings.

I changed phone. Is my data transferred?

Pacto stores data locally on your device only, with no server and no account. To move to a new phone, use the backup feature: export a backup file from the app (Mail, iCloud Drive, AirDrop...) and import it on the new phone. Your purchase can be restored with "Restore purchases". Without a backup file, data cannot be recovered.

How do I delete my data?

In the app, go to Settings and tap "Reset PACTO on this device" to erase all data stored by Pacto, or simply uninstall the app. We hold no copy of your data on any server.

The message to my child doesn't open WhatsApp.

WhatsApp is optional. Make sure it is installed, or copy the message or show the brief on your screen and announce the pacto to your child in person.

Which languages are supported?

French, English, Spanish and Arabic.

I found a bug or have a suggestion.
