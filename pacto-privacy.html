Pacto — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026. Publisher: Abdellatif BNAYA, ASY-TECH SOLUTIONS. Contact: yart-deco@outlook.fr

Summary

Pacto has no account, no advertising and no analytics or tracking. We do not collect your personal data on any server.

Data stored on your device

Child profiles (first name, age, and optionally a photo, school, class and a phone number used only to open a message), pactos and their progress are stored locally on your device only. Photos you choose stay on your device. This data is never sent to us. In Settings, "Reset PACTO on this device" erases it, as does uninstalling the app. An optional PIN code is kept in the iOS secure keychain on your device.

Camera, photo library, Face ID

The camera and photo library are used only if you choose to add a photo to a profile. Face ID / biometrics, if enabled, only lock the app locally; we never receive biometric data.

Reminders and backups

Pacto can schedule local reminder notifications on your device; they are generated by the app itself and do not involve any server. You can export a backup file and import it later; the file is created on your device and shared only where you choose (Mail, iCloud Drive, AirDrop...). We never receive it.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to verify and restore purchase entitlements. RevenueCat may process an anonymous identifier and purchase status; it does not receive your child's data. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Messages

When you send a pacto to your child, the app opens WhatsApp (or another messaging app) with a pre-filled message. You choose the recipient and whether to send. The message is then handled by that app under its own policy.

Images and updates

Some decorative images are loaded from Unsplash when you are online, which may expose your IP address to that service. App updates may be delivered through Expo (expo-updates), which receives technical information such as app version.

Children

Pacto is intended for parents. Children do not use the app and we do not knowingly collect any data from children.

Your rights

Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing to export or erase on our side. For any question, write to yart-deco@outlook.fr.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change.

Politique de confidentialité (résumé en français)

Pacto n'a ni compte, ni publicité, ni suivi. Les profils et pactos sont stockés uniquement sur votre appareil. Les achats sont gérés par Apple (via RevenueCat). L'envoi du message à l'enfant se fait par WhatsApp à votre initiative. Pour effacer vos données : Réglages → « Réinitialiser PACTO sur cet appareil », ou désinstallez l'app. Contact : yart-deco@outlook.fr.
